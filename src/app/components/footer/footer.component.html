Privacy Policy

Who We Are

Tunicure is a Category A travel agency and medical tourism coordination company based in Hammam Sousse, Tunisia. We do not provide medical care. All medical consultations, diagnoses, and treatments are carried out exclusively by properly licensed clinics and doctors in Tunisia.

Our role is limited to organising travel, accommodation, appointments, and facilitating communication between patients and medical providers.

Scope of This Policy

This policy explains how Tunicure collects, uses, stores, and shares personal and medical data in accordance with Tunisian law and recognised international best practices, including principles of the UK/EU GDPR where applicable to international clients.

By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

Data We Collect

  • Identity and contact details (name, email, phone, nationality, language)
  • Medical data and photographs voluntarily submitted
  • Travel-related information
  • Technical data (IP address, device info, cookies)
  • Payment data processed by secure third-party providers (no card storage)

How We Use Your Data

  • Organise medical tourism services and appointments
  • Transmit medical information to partner clinics and doctors
  • Manage travel and stay in Tunisia
  • Communicate regarding your request
  • Improve services and comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We may share your data only with:

  • Licensed clinics and doctors
  • Travel and accommodation partners
  • IT, hosting and payment providers
  • Authorities where required by law

We never sell personal data.

International Transfers & Retention

Data may be processed in Tunisia and other countries involved in the service with appropriate safeguards. Data is retained only as long as necessary and securely deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of your data.

Contact: contact@tunicure.com

Cookies

We use cookies to ensure proper functioning of the website, analyse usage and, where applicable, for marketing. Preferences can be managed via the cookie banner or browser settings.

Medical Tourism Disclaimer

Medical Data & Consent

Medical data is sensitive personal data. By submitting information or photographs, you give explicit consent for its use for medical evaluation and coordination of care.

Where technically possible, medical files are transmitted directly to treating doctors and are not used by non-medical staff. You confirm that the information you provide is accurate and complete.

Medical Responsibility

Tunicure is not a healthcare provider and does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, or guarantees of results. All medical acts are performed under the sole responsibility of the clinics and doctors.

Medical outcomes depend on your individual condition and the professional judgement of healthcare providers.

Foreign Currency Declaration

Any import or export of foreign currency equal to or exceeding the equivalent of 20,000 Tunisian dinars must be declared to customs upon entry, exit or transit.

  • Re-export over 5,000 TND requires a stamped import declaration
  • Re-export over 30,000 TND must be done via authorised banks
  • Declaration valid for 3 months and one trip only
  • Declaration is personal and non-transferable

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version published on the website applies.
