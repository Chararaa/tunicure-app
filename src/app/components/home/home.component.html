About Tunicure

What Is Tunicure ?

Tunicure is a medical travel agency based in Sousse, on Tunisia’s Sahel coast specialised in organising high-end cosmetic surgery treatments in Tunisia. We provide a fully personalised, VIP medical travel experience, taking care of our patients from start to finish, with professionalism, discretion, and attention to detail. From the very first consultation to post-treatment follow-up, Tunicure manages every aspect of your journey so you can focus entirely on your health, comfort, and results.

Our Areas of Expertise

Tunicure specialises in a wide range of medical treatments, including:

Cosmetic surgery (body contouring, breast surgery, facial surgery, rhinoplasty, etc.)

Eye laser treatments (vision correction)

Hair transplant and hair restoration

Dental treatments (implants, veneers, smile makeovers) Each treatment plan is tailored to your medical needs and expectations.
